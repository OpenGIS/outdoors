# Style Structure

> Outdoors is a thin outdoor modification on the [OpenGIS Basemap](https://www.ogis.org/basemap/) — the OpenMapTiles OSM style pointed at OpenFreeMap planet tiles, fetched from `https://www.ogis.org/basemap/style.json` and cached at build time.

[![Style Comparison](../screenshots/dolomites.jpg)](https://www.ogis.org/outdoors/#13/45.81321/11.74098)

## The Basemap Foundation

The OpenGIS Basemap (published at <https://www.ogis.org/basemap/>, upstream repo: [opengis/basemap](https://github.com/opengis/basemap)) provides an excellent world map featuring:

- Vector [OpenStreetMap](https://www.openstreetmap.org/) data ([OpenMapTiles](http://openmaptiles.org/) schema).
- Human-readable layer ids (`Grass`, `Water`, `Footway path`, `Zoo`, `Mountain peak labels`…).
- Self-hosted glyphs and sprite at `www.ogis.org/basemap` (the sprite is re-wrapped as the `default` sheet of a two-sheet array — see [Sprites & Icons](12.sprites.md)).
- Tiles at `tiles.openfreemap.org/planet`.
- Attribution — build-owned via the [`ATTRIBUTION`](../scripts/build.mjs) block: the basemap string is synchronised onto both the live `openmaptiles` source and the basemap's `attribution` pseudo-source; never strip it (licence requirement).

The build orders its modifications through a single bottom-to-top [`RENDER_STACK`](../scripts/build.mjs) array of render bands, walked by `applyModifications()` (see [Render Stack](10.render-stack.md)).

Outdoors keeps most of the basemap's rendering and applies outdoor modifications on top — recolouring the core canvas, natural-earth landcover/landuse, water and park palettes, adding the DEM hillshade, contours and the two outdoor-POI layers, restyling roads and paths with the three low-zoom path layers, and layering the waymarked hiking- and cycling-route linework with badges on the marked networks and mixed green where the activities share a way. When the satellite ground feature is enabled it also blends the Esri World Imagery raster over the natural-earth vectors — above the calibrated landcover/landuse fills, beneath the water and every later layer — so the vector ground reads through, and the late imagery legibility pass recedes the contours, tones the man-made surfaces, swaps the base `Building` fill for `building-outline` and adds the `building-3d` massing (see [Ground Raster](11.ground-raster.md)).

> [!IMPORTANT]
> The style is generated programmatically; see [Build Script](1.build.md). Each feature has a toggle; flip it and rebuild (this is automatic in the dev app).

## Modification Groups

The modification functions live alongside the config in [`scripts/build.mjs`](../scripts/build.mjs); [`applyModifications()`](../scripts/build.mjs) only walks the bottom-to-top [`RENDER_STACK`](../scripts/build.mjs) of render bands and then runs [`FINISHING_PASSES`](../scripts/build.mjs) for cross-group overrides, gated by `FEATURES` toggles. The groups below follow the band order; see [Render Stack](10.render-stack.md) for the full group table.

- **Core canvas & natural-earth** — recolours the `Background` floor and the calibrated landcover/landuse fills, plus park differentiation; under the raster the vectors are retained and the imagery composites over them (`applyCorePalette()`, `applyNaturalEarthPalette()`, `applyParkDifferentiation()`). See [Natural Earth](9.natural-earth.md).
- **Ground raster** — when enabled, the `esri-satellite` raster source and `satellite` layer blend Esri World Imagery over the natural-earth vectors, inserted at the first water layer so the raster sits above the vectors and below the water, and painted at a zoom-ramped `raster-opacity`. `applyGroundRetention()` strips `SAT_STRIP_LAYERS`, `applyWaterTint()` makes the water fills translucent, and the late `applyImageryLegibility()` pass adapts contours, labels, buildings and man-made surfaces (`applySatelliteGround()`, `applyGroundRetention()`, `applyWaterTint()`, `applyImageryLegibility()`). See [Ground Raster](11.ground-raster.md).
- **Water palette** — recolours water fills & lines (`applyWaterPalette()`).
- **DEM** — the `demSource` raster-dem, the `hillshade-layer` inserted above landcover/landuse and below water, and the style's `terrain` property at 1.2 exaggeration (`applyDemSource()`, `applyDemHillshade()`, `applyDemTerrain()`). See [Elevation & Terrain](5.dem.md) and [Performance](8.performance.md).
- **Contours** — the contour source, `contour-lines` and `contour-labels` elevation labels (`applyContours()`, `applyContourLabels()`). See [Elevation & Terrain](5.dem.md).
- **Road surface awareness** — a paved/unpaved hierarchy on the basemap's road fill layers: tiered asphalt greys, neutral lighter casings, surface-aware widths, lighter unpaved ways and faded tunnels (`applyRoadSurfaceAware()`). See [Build Script](1.build.md).
- **Path styling** — the basemap's Footway/Bridleway/Cycleway/Steps path family unified into the outdoor trail look, gated to z14+ (`applyPathStyling()`). See [Paths](3.paths.md).
- **Low-zoom paths** — the `outdoor-paths` overlay owning z9–13 (`applyLowZoomPaths()`). See [Paths](3.paths.md).
- **Waymarked routes** — the `outdoor-routes` overlay in the `routes` band between `borders` and `labels-poi`, with hiking and cycling tier families from one `ROUTE_ACTIVITIES` registry, ivory casings, `ref`/`name` badges on marked networks and mixed green on ways shared by both enabled activities (`applyRouteOverlay()`). Cycling draws beneath hiking. See [Activities](7.activities.md).
- **Outdoor POIs** — the config-driven `outdoor-poi` overlay plus the `outdoor-amenities` planet layer (`applyOutdoorPoi()`). See [Outdoor POIs](4.pois.md).

## Labels

- **Peak labels** are native to the basemap: `Mountain peak labels` and `Volcano peak labels` (name + elevation at z12+, with peak/volcano icons).
- **Contour labels** are inserted just below the peak label layers, so contours beat POI labels in collisions but yield to peaks.
- **Park labels** — `Local park` and `National park labels` are recoloured to a dark green.

## Outdoor Overlays

- **Low-zoom paths** — fills the z9–13 gap; see [Paths](3.paths.md).
- **Outdoor POIs** — overlay plus planet amenities; see [Outdoor POIs](4.pois.md).
- **Waymarked routes** — one `outdoor-routes` source, two activity tier families, ivory casings, `ref`/`name` badges, mixed green on shared ways; see [Activities](7.activities.md).

Every outdoor colour is a `palette.json` token exposed to the build as `COLOURS`, and the route shields are drawn from the palette-rendered outdoors sprite; see [Build Script](1.build.md#palette-workflow).

## Sprites & Icons

Outdoors layers its own `outdoors` sprite sheet over the basemap's `default` sheet; the style's `sprite` property is a two-entry `{id, url}` array written by `build()` so both sheets resolve wherever the style is hosted. Icons resolve as `<id>:<name>` for a named sheet or bare `<name>` against `default`; the outdoors inventory is derived from [`palette.json`](../palette.json)'s `icons` registry into `OUTDOOR_SPRITE_ICONS`, and the build's basemap-POI rewrite lands every `icon-image` on a loaded icon. `npm run check:pois` enforces the per-sheet resolution and orphan rules. See [Sprites & Icons](12.sprites.md).

The sheet is a raster PNG, not an SDF atlas: `spreet` rasterises the resolved SVG fills into the PNG, so `icon-color`/`icon-halo-*` paint can neither recolour a glyph nor draw a halo around it. Glyph colours are palette-driven: each icon in `palette.json` names `primary` and optional `detail` roles in `iconRoles`, and a role is a hex value or an `@map.<TOKEN>` reference into the `map` block. The route shields use the `route-hiking`, `route-cycling` and `route-mixed` roles, which reference `@map.ROUTES.*` so their fills track the route linework. The authored sources in `icons/` carry `__primary__`/`__detail__` fill slots; `scripts/palette.mjs` substitutes the roles into gitignored `build/icons/`, and `npm run sprite:build` rasterises them with `spreet` and rewrites the committed `sprite.lock.json`.
