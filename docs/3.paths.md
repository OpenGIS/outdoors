# Paths

> Shifting focus from road networks to navigation by foot.

[![Paths](../screenshots/sunshine-coast.png)](https://www.ogis.org/outdoors/#11.93/49.97661/-124.45956)

## Promotion

[Liberty](https://github.com/maputnik/osm-liberty) displays footpaths (`road_path_pedestrian`) as white dotted lines, which against a light green background can be hard to see. Outdoors restyles `road_path_pedestrian` lines and labels to bright red, so they stand out. Tracks and minor roads are also styled to be more prominent.

While these now stand out, they are only included in the source [OpenFreeMap](https://openfreemap.org/) tiles at higher zooms. In practice this means that paths/tracks are available for navigation when you are zoomed in on your location, but if you zoom out to view your surroundings, they disappear: below z14 only route-member paths remain, and below z12 none at all.

This is due to the [OpenMapTiles Schema](https://github.com/openmaptiles/openmaptiles), which omits these geometries in order to reduce tile file size (references: [Show track/path sooner](https://github.com/openmaptiles/openmaptiles/issues/271), [Selective rendering of tracks and paths at z12-13](https://github.com/openmaptiles/openmaptiles/pull/1190)).

## Low Zoom Paths

In order to make paths visible at lower zooms, a custom extract is created from the planet [OpenStreetMap](https://www.openstreetmap.org/) [dataset](https://www.geofabrik.de/) using [Planetiler](https://github.com/onthegomap/planetiler) and added to the map as an additional vector source (`outdoor-paths`). This path/footway/track geometry is generated with Planetiler and served from the Open GIS `tile.ogis.app/paths` and adds low-traffic, largely off-road ways from z9–13.

See [Tile Server](7.server.md).
